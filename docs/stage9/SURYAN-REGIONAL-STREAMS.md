## Nagercoil launch evidence

The [AirMedia report](https://airmedia.in/news/radio-listeners-of-kanyakumari-district-listen-listen-keep-on-listening-fm-radio-network-with-the-launch-of-suryan-fm-91-9-mhz-in-nagercoil-tamil-nadu) explicitly bears **18 August 2026**, reports **91.9 MHz**, and describes the eleventh Suryan station. AirMedia is a trade source, not AIR/Prasar Bharati. A [SuryanFM-branded public post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/suryanfm_nagercoil-get-ready-to-tune-in-to-your-city-activity-7494581203463860224-eGfq) separately announces Nagercoil 91.9. Account control and the post's exact calendar date were not independently authenticated; displayed relative dates must not become a fabricated launch day.

The [official About page](https://www.suryanfm.in/about-us/) names the earlier ten-city footprint and [Sun's radio page](https://www.sun.in/radio.aspx) describes localized programming. Their older lists are incomplete evidence for the newer launch. The [March 2026 regulator list](https://mib.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-03/390_operationalchannels_as-pn-30032026.pdf) predates August and cannot disprove it. The archived `suryan-nagercoil` ID and 91.9 value remain intact; no Internet transmitter equivalence is inferred.

## Candidate outcome

[TingFM](https://tingfm.com/radio/25900) publishes `https://tamil.crabdance.com:8002/2`. Two later separate deep audit sessions timed out; no successful HTTP/audio result or city identity was established. The local guide's `https://radios.crabdance.com:8002/1` failed certificate validation; verification was not disabled. Radio Browser contains historical HTTP mounts 1, 2 and 3 under Chennai labels; mount 3 is also labelled Hello Chennai. **Neither the server ports nor those labels prove a broadcaster-authorized relay.** No HTTP scheme was silently upgraded and no unauthorized proxy was used.

No official player mapping was established for independent Chennai, Coimbatore, Nagercoil or other city online broadcasts. A single authorized network-wide feed was also not established. This is an inconclusive bounded investigation, not proof that Suryan offers none anywhere. No shared URL was assigned to all city records.

## Use review and next actions

[Suryan's terms](https://www.suryanfm.in/terms-of-use/) contain personal/noncommercial access provisions and restrictions on reuse/deep-linking/streaming beyond the stated license. An open third-party relay is not a RadioBee grant. Keep all candidates in Group C until transport, programme ownership/city scope and applicable use conditions are resolved. Seek an official player configuration and reviewed allowance directly from the broadcaster; no messages were sent on the user's behalf. Reconcile current Suryan branding with older regulator RED labels for Erode/Salem/Vellore before any alias or restored card.
